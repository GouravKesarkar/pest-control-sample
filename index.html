Common questions

Is the treatment safe for children and pets?

We use low-odour products approved by the Central Insecticides Board (CIB&RC). Keep children and pets away from treated areas until dry, usually 2 to 3 hours.

How long does a treatment take?

Most homes take 1 to 3 hours depending on size and pest type.

Do I need to vacate my home?

Usually not. For fogging or bed bug treatment we advise staying out for a few hours.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Termite treatment comes with a written warranty and other services include free follow-up visits.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on the pest, area and service. Inspection is free and you get a fixed quote before work begins.
